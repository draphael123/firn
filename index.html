    The rite

    How it is carried

    You do not steer the ice. You tilt the world. Hold a direction and the ground holds that angle — release and it levels. WASD or the arrow keys; a gamepad stick if you have one.

    Speed is heat. Friction and sunlight melt the shell, and the shell is the only thing keeping the thing inside asleep. Watch it clear: when you can see what you are carrying, you have gone too far.

    A thinner shell is smaller. Some gates admit only a ball that has melted down — so you may spend shell deliberately to buy a shortcut. But a grate that held you at full thickness will not hold you when you are thin.

    When the shell is gone you keep rolling, bare and quick, one hard blow from waking it. Reach the ring and set it down.

    The road climbs. Warm air sits low, so every stage is partly a matter of getting above it — but the thaw is rising behind you faster than you are climbing, and each stage begins in worse air than the last.
